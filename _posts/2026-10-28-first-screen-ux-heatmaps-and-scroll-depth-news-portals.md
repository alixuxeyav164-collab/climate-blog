# Measuring First-Screen Click Density and Scroll Depth on Mobile and Desktop News Layouts

*By **[Oleksandr Savchuk](https://pulspolski24.pl/autor-marek-kowalczyk/)** ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B4%D1%80-%D1%81%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%87%D1%83%D0%BA-342726447/)) - SEO Specialist at [Puls Polski 24](https://pulspolski24.pl/)*

When auditing page experience and user engagement metrics with tools like [Plerdy](https://www.plerdy.com/), three above-the-fold factors consistently separate low-bounce news portals from high-bounce sites:

1. **Immediate Visual Hierarchy:** Placing the #1 lead story (*Temat Dnia*) and the numbered *Top 4* list directly below the breaking news bar without intermediate card grids.
2. **Strict WebP Asset Budgets:** Keeping all logos and hero thumbnails under 45-65 KB in `.webp` format so Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) stays below 1.5 seconds on 4G mobile connections.
3. **Contextual Internal Linking (`Czytaj takze`):** Inserting 3 relevant internal links mid-article so readers naturally transition from a breaking news story to a deep calculation guide.

Explore our live implementation at [Puls Polski 24](https://pulspolski24.pl/) and our Ukrainian municipal portal [Kyianyn](https://kyianyn.com.ua/).
